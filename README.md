RKTRL (ReKontrol) — MIDI controller, sequencer and live effects for Android

RKTRL turns an Android phone or tablet into a control center for your synths, drum machines and other MIDI gear, over Bluetooth MIDI or USB. The app is totally free.

// CONTROLLER

128 assignable knobs (4 banks of 32): MIDI channel, CC number and value range for each.
One fader drives one or multiple knobs at once (GROUP mode).
No value jumps: the fader picks up the instrument’s real value before changing it.
FORCE mode to set the value directly, and LATCH to choose whether the fader stays put or glides back to centre.
UNDO in one tap.

// SEQUENCER

16 tracks, up to 1024 steps per track, with an A and B page for live variations.
On every step: note, velocity, length, chords, trig conditions, and ratchets.
Long-press a step for pan, modulation, pitch bend, accent and slide.
Each track has its own length, division, transpose and play direction (forward, reverse, ping-pong, random).
Generators: AUTO (bassline, lead, acid, pad, drone, arp, techno, Berlin school, riff, djent, EBM, solo), two-layer EUCLIDEAN and scale-based RANDOM.
Copy / paste tracks, live mute, swing, MIDI clock out.

// DESTROY MODE

A Kaoss-style X/Y pad with 9 live MIDI effects: Repeat, Stutter, Echo, Filter, Pitch, Tape Stop, Scatter, Octaver, Warp.
Use effects alone or combined (MULTI), on the MIDI channels you choose.
Everything returns to normal as soon as you lift your finger.
Geometric visuals on screen, 8 assignable pads and TAP BPM.

// ALSO

Save and reload your whole setup.
Custom names for your 16 MIDI channels.
Pick your interface colours, in French or English.
